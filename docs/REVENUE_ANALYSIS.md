## 💰 Revenue Model Comparison

### Mobile App Revenue Streams

#### 1. **Paid App Model**
```
Price: $4.99 - $9.99 one-time purchase
Conservative: 500 downloads/year × $4.99 = $2,495/year
Moderate: 2,000 downloads/year × $6.99 = $13,980/year
Optimistic: 5,000 downloads/year × $9.99 = $49,950/year

Reality Check: Most B2B tools get 100-1,000 downloads in year 1
Expected: $2,000 - $7,000/year
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Immediate revenue per download
- ✅ Simple, no ongoing relationship needed
- ✅ Users who pay are committed

**Cons:**
- ❌ High barrier to entry (most won't pay upfront)
- ❌ No recurring revenue
- ❌ Hard to compete with free alternatives

#### 2. **Freemium + In-App Purchases**
```
Free Download + Premium Features

Free Tier: Unlimited users
- Basic calculations (up to 3 scenarios/day)
- No data export
- Ads included

Premium ($2.99/month or $24.99/year):
- Unlimited scenarios
- PDF/Excel export
- Save scenarios
- Cost analysis tools
- No ads
- Priority support

Revenue Projection:
- 5,000 free downloads
- 5% convert to premium = 250 paying users
- 250 × $24.99/year = $6,247.50/year

More Realistic (Year 1):
- 1,000 downloads
- 3% conversion = 30 premium users
- 30 × $24.99 = $749.70/year

With Growth (Year 2-3):
- 10,000 downloads
- 5% conversion = 500 users
- 500 × $24.99 = $12,495/year
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Low barrier to entry (free to try)
- ✅ Recurring revenue
- ✅ Can upsell existing users
- ✅ Higher lifetime value

**Cons:**
- ❌ Slow initial revenue
- ❌ Need to manage subscriptions
- ❌ Must provide ongoing value

#### 3. **Ad-Supported Model**
```
Free app with ads

Banner Ads: $0.50 - $2 per 1,000 impressions (CPM)
Interstitial Ads: $3 - $10 per 1,000 impressions

Conservative:
- 1,000 active users
- 5 sessions/month per user
- 5,000 ad impressions/month
- $1 CPM = $5/month = $60/year

Moderate:
- 5,000 active users
- 10 sessions/month
- 50,000 impressions/month
- $2 CPM = $100/month = $1,200/year

Realistic for B2B Tool (Year 1): $200 - $800/year
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Free to users
- ✅ Scales with usage
- ✅ No payment friction

**Cons:**
- ❌ Very low revenue per user
- ❌ Unprofessional for business tool
- ❌ Annoys users
- ❌ Need massive scale
