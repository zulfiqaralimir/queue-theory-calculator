ℹ️ How It Works

This calculator uses the M/M/c queue model (Erlang C formula) to determine optimal staffing levels.

Stochastic modeling means it accounts for the random, unpredictable nature of customer arrivals.

The algorithm calculates the minimum staff needed to prevent infinite queues, then finds the optimal number to meet your wait time target.
